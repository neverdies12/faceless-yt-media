Mind-Blowing Facts

Mind-Blowing Facts is a content service that produces short, original educational fact videos — science, history, and psychology, always accurate, never sensationalized — and publishes them daily to a small number of connected creator accounts across YouTube, TikTok, and Instagram on the account owner's behalf.

How it works

Every video follows the same production process, end to end:

  1. Writing. Each script is written specifically for that video — never copied or paraphrased from another creator's content — and fact-checked against the original claim before it goes into production.
  2. Production. A narrated voiceover is paired with licensed stock footage and burned-in captions to produce a finished vertical video, ready for YouTube Shorts, TikTok, and Instagram Reels.
  3. Connecting an account. A creator connects one or more of their social accounts through that platform's own official OAuth sign-in flow. No account is ever accessed without its owner explicitly authorizing the connection first.
  4. Publishing. Once connected, new videos are published to that creator's account automatically going forward. A creator can disconnect at any time, which immediately stops any further publishing to that account.

Who it's for: right now, a small number of creators who wanted a hands-off way to keep a "daily facts" channel active without producing every video themselves. It's not a public self-serve signup yet — see the Contact section below to inquire about connecting an account.

Our approach to content

Every fact published is checked for accuracy before it's used — the goal is content that's genuinely surprising without ever being misleading. Scripts are original writing, not reused or lightly edited from another source, and stock footage is used under its full commercial license. This matters both for our own credibility and because most platforms' monetization and distribution policies specifically penalize reused or low-effort content — originality isn't optional here, it's how the whole thing works.

Frequently asked questions

Is this a bot posting spam?

No. Every video is an original, fact-checked script produced specifically for that post. Nothing is reposted, scraped, or copied from other accounts.

What data do you collect from a connected account?

Only what's needed to publish on that account's behalf — an account identifier and the access token issued by that platform's own OAuth flow. See our Privacy Policy for full detail.

Can I disconnect my account?

Yes, at any time, either from your own account's connected-apps settings on that platform, or by asking us directly. Disconnecting stops all further publishing to that account immediately.

Is the content the same across every connected account?

Currently yes — the same daily fact video is published to each connected creator's account. This may change as the service grows.

How do I get my account connected?

This isn't a public self-serve product yet. Reach out using the contact details below.
